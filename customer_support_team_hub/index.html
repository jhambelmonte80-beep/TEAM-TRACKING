Performance metrics to track

  • First response time (FRT)
  • Resolution time and first-contact resolution
  • Customer satisfaction (CSAT), with response count
  • Quality assurance score and review sample size
  • Backlog, reopen rate and escalation themes
  • Schedule adherence and coverage gaps

Reporting guardrails

  • Always show the reporting period and data source.
  • Compare like-for-like ticket types and channels.
  • Never rank agents on speed without quality context.
  • Use small samples cautiously; explain limitations.
  • Share trends and recommended actions, not blame.
